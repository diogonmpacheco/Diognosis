Diognosis is an on-device medication and pharmacogenomic review workspace. It checks selected medicines, supplements, foods, optional gene results, timing, metabolites, and source-linked evidence together.
How to use it
1
Add items to the listSearch or browse for medications, supplements, foods, or clinical context items.
2
Add gene results only if knownGene and marker results are optional. Leave them blank unless there is a real report.
3
Review Safety Notes firstUse the notes to prepare questions for a doctor or pharmacist. Switch to Clinician when more technical detail is needed.
What it can help surface
Safety notes to discuss before changing anything
Gene and metabolite effects that may matter if results are known
Timing, persistence, washout, and level-change context
Evidence links and boundaries for doctor or pharmacist follow-up
Boundary: Diognosis is for education and medication-safety review. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, prescribing guidance, or proof that a list is safe. No personal data is uploaded or sent to runtime APIs.
