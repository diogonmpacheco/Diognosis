ClinPGx and CPIC Data are never queried from the browser. Their records are cached/staged at script time and remain pending review until a human promotes a specific record.

CPIC records report whether they are `local_review_candidate_not_fetched` or `fetched_from_source`. ClinPGx records report whether they are direct `fetched_from_source` API records or `derived_from_open_targets_snapshot` context.
