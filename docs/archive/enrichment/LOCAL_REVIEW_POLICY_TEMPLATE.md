## Decision Policy

- Local reviews are local/fork-scoped.
- Local reviews do not change upstream professional-review status.
- Local scoring, if enabled by a fork, must be labeled as local.
- Public severity in upstream Diognosis must not be changed by a local overlay.

## Required Checks

- Source faithfulness.
- Drug/gene/metabolite mapping.
- Directionality.
- License/copyright boundary.
- Professional role and scope.
- Date and reviewer identity.
